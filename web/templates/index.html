Analyzing

Method

  1. 01

    Frequency features

    Each 8×8 block is described by a short DCT signature.

  2. 02

    Block matching

    Similar signatures are paired, ignoring flat areas that would match by chance.

  3. 03

    Shift voting

    Pairs that share the same displacement are kept as a copied region.

  4. 04

    Cleanup

    Small isolated marks are removed so the remaining mask is the duplicated area.
